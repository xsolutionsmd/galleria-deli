Build type: landing. Endpoint: full, production and two independent previews.

- Catering phone inquiry. Date, group size and food preferences can be practical visitor prompts; no invented packages or required notice.

No custom backend, payment, booking, account, admin or misleading delivery form. Static health/version/noindex runtime and destination checks required. No real customer transaction in tests.
