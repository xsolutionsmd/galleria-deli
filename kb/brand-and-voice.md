# Brand and voice

Exact public name: Galleria Deli. Official positioning emphasizes familiar local service, scratch preparation and fresh roast turkey. [S1]

Use clear, warm, restrained copy about breakfast, sandwiches, Korean bowls, catering inquiries and visiting. Avoid fine-dining claims, invented packages, superlatives and delivery promises. Actual business imagery should inform palette and composition. A text wordmark is appropriate without a verified original logo.
