# Design brief

## Visitor needs and page map

A nearby resident or office visitor should find food, see the complete menu, ask about catering and reach the deli.

- Home: identity, owners' 2005 start, scratch preparation, turkey and Korean-inspired highlights; real imagery and menu/call actions. [S1]
- Menu: all 54 published items, ten categories, descriptions, prices and seasonal soup/salad invitation from menu-source.json. [S1]
- Catering: phone inquiry and concise practical planning prompts; no invented packages, lead times, online quote submission or delivery promise. [S1]
- Visit: address, published hours, call and directions. [S1][S2]

## Standing design rules

Separate page URLs, restrained consistent typography, actual assets visibly rendered. No decorative numbering/counters/photo prefixes, preview banners, fake testimonials or unsupported badges. SVG arrows. Useful whitespace, keyboard focus, reduced motion, narrow-phone and enlarged-text card/control containment. Subtle purposeful animation. Honest uncertainty without cluttered copy.
